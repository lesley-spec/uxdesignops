# Integrity — Test loopback

## Verified vs AI

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Verified** | Direct session evidence — quote, task outcome, recording timestamp, Maze metric |
| **AI interpretation** | Theming, clustering, recommendation narrative |

Only **Verified** rows belong in published Confluence/Jira research context. AI interpretation stays in draft or appendix until designer confirms.

## Not loopback input

- `test-plan` drafts (pre-recruiting)
- `ux-critique` / `accessibility-review` synthetic pre-tests
- Model recall without session source

## Human gate

Designer confirms the loopback pack before publish. Stakeholder summaries must trace surprises to Verified evidence.

## PII

Anonymize quotes. Do not paste participant emails or full names unless the destination is approved for PII.

## Marketplace

Tag findings **Brand**, **Partner**, or **Shared**. Do not generalize Partner pain as Brand insight without stating the gap.
