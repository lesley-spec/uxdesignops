Claims

Confidence is about independence, not sample size: HIGH needs two sources that agree, MEDIUM is one good source however large its n, LOW is anecdotal or ageing. Hover any badge for the full bar.

Unmet needs

What people need and do not have, placed where it bites in their journey. Severity is how much it hurts; evidence is how well we know. The two come apart often, and the gap between them is the useful part: high severity on thin evidence is a research job, not a design job. Each need links to the claims it rests on \u2014 follow one to the Dovetail study or PostHog funnel behind it.
