How this report is calculated

Combines AI Search Visibility mentions, observed publisher retrievals (ScalePost today; impact.com crawler telemetry in pilot), advertiser AI-origin visits, and commerce linkage. Retrieval ≠ mention. Evertune alone does not qualify a partner for compensation. Index is for ranking and bonus eligibility — not path credit.

Current limitations: retrieval-to-sales correlation in DS is early (0.2–0.3). Halo / AI Equity readout 2.6x is a DS experiment, not this index. Sparse partners show Insufficient evidence, not “low influence.”
