Define — frame the problem, not the feature

One-pager + UX epic, after anti-sycophancy and dual marketplace lenses.

design-thinking-ux (master) write-spec anti-sycophancy brand-operator-ux-persona creator-affiliate-ux-persona design-system

Framing (labeled)

  • Verified: 74.68% drop details → send.
  • AI interpretation: Partners stall while deciding if a proposal is worth sending. Details shows metadata; commission lives on Promote. No persistent Send CTA.
  • Goal (not a feature): Partners who open details can decide and send in the same visit when they intend to.

Pressure test

  • A louder button is not the goal.
  • Brand ACMs receive proposals — making Send easier may raise low-quality inbound.
  • 16m median could be reading terms, abandoning, or a second session. We do not know yet.

Test — draft a study, do not fake findings

Synthetic pre-flight, then an Outset study draft. No recruiting. No loopback.

design-thinking-ux (master) accessibility-review ux-critique test-plan test-loopback skipped

Study draft (Outset, not Maze)

  • Moderated — we need to hear why people stall.
  • Recruit Partner / creator-affiliates who considered a proposal in the last 90 days. N = 5–8.
  • Tasks: decide if a program is worth proposing; name commission and deal runtime without switching tabs; send a proposal.

A11y pre-flight (synthetic)

  • No WCAG blockers for a pipeline demo.
  • Major: send modal has no focus trap; prev/next chevrons still unlabeled (pre-existing).
  • Duplicate Send CTAs are intentional (D1).
  • This is not a conformance audit.
Human gate — and a hard stop test-loopback publishes Verified findings to Confluence and Jira. It must not run on synthetic pre-tests. This walkthrough correctly did not publish fake session findings.
