Define — frame the problem, not the feature

One-pager + UX epic, after anti-sycophancy and dual marketplace lenses.

design-thinking-ux (master) write-spec anti-sycophancy brand-operator-ux-persona creator-affiliate-ux-persona design-system

Framing (labeled)

  • Verified: impact.com cannot bill publisher accounts; dummy advertiser workaround; Option 1 = debit earnings on existing invoices.
  • AI interpretation: The pain is not “add invoices.” A Partner who already earns must become a Brand to pay us — splitting identity, permissions, and cash.
  • Goal (not a feature): A billed publisher stays on one Partner account, understands what they owe, and settles from earnings.

Pressure test

  • An invoice list is not the goal. Ending the two-account split is.
  • Brand already has funding accounts — do not collapse this into “users need invoices.”
  • Option 2 (wallet) and Option 3 (rev-share at clearing) are future. Shipping them as MVP is scope creep Greg explicitly parked.
  • Insufficient earnings is an open product decision (block withdrawal vs collections) — do not hide it.

Test — draft a study, do not fake findings

Synthetic pre-flight, then a Maze / Outset study draft. No recruiting. No loopback.

design-thinking-ux (master) accessibility-review ux-critique test-plan test-loopback skipped

Study draft (Maze unmoderated + Outset hybrid)

  • Maze 20–40 for task success; Outset 5–8 Expert/Intermediate publishers with fee contracts for the insufficient-funds story.
  • Tasks: find the fee due on Balance; explain available vs reserved in one sentence; pay from earnings; find the PDF; hit the short-balance state.
  • Success = stay on one account and complete pay without asking for a “billing advertiser.”

A11y pre-flight (synthetic)

  • No WCAG blockers for a pipeline demo.
  • Major to watch: slide-out focus return, labelled Pay button, contrast on minus amounts.
  • Insufficient-funds copy must not rely on color alone.
  • This is not a conformance audit.
Human gate — and a hard stop test-loopback publishes Verified findings to Confluence and Jira. It must not run on synthetic pre-tests. This walkthrough correctly did not publish fake session findings.
