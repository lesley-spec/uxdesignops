What to take from Brand this month

About 38k Brand accounts exist; only 8,120 (21%) opened the web app in the last 30 days. Those actives are sticky — 69% weekly/monthly — and live in Engage. Discover is the friction hotspot (extreme marketplace U-turns). Optimize and Protect are niche. Design for the weekly operator in Engage, not the dormant 79%.

Findings

Verified · high friction

Marketplace / Discover U-turns

4,155 Brand accounts used Discover (51% of actives) and logged 401k U-turns — about 97 per account. Marketplace pages: 128k U-turns among 3,495 accounts. Performance by Partner: 20.8k rage clicks.

AI interpretation: operators search, back out, and retry in partner discovery and that report. Duplicate Pendo page tags inflate some counts — treat magnitude as directional until tagging is cleaned.

Verified

Engage is the Brand home

6,710 accounts (83% of actives) used Engage. Dashboard, Partners, Applications, Template Terms, and Performance by Partner are the real Brand IA. Optimize (6%) and Protect (11%) are specialist surfaces.

Verified

Small active core, high stickiness

21% of Brand accounts visited in 30 days; those who did came back weekly (69% WAU/MAU) and spent ~18 minutes per active day. About 2.9 visitors per active account — ACM / operator teams, not single-player.

Recommendations

Do thisBecause
1Put Marketplace / Find Partners and Performance-by-Partner on the next critique or test plan — search, filters, empty states, back-out paths.Highest U-turn and rage-click pages among Brand actives.
2Treat Engage as the default Brand shell. Keep Optimize/Protect out of primary IA unless the spec is for those products.83% of actives are in Engage; Optimize is 6%.
3Design for a weekly operator with ~3 people per account, not a first-run advertiser.69% WAU/MAU; 21% of the book is the real user base this month.
4Do not spin a “get Brands to log in” initiative from this snapshot.The active core is stable week to week. Dormant 79% is a CRM/activation question.

What to take from Partner this month

3.06M Partner accounts exist; 216k (7%) visited in 30 days. Most who show up hit Checklist or Home, then Find Brands. Link & Content Tools reached 1% of actives. Month-1 retention is 15% versus 47% on Brand. Unique monthly accounts jumped 38% versus the prior 30 days — watch that in September before calling it a product win.

Findings

Verified

Activation still dominates the membersite

Partner Checklist is the #1 page: 128,075 accounts (59% of 30-day actives). Home Dashboard is second (100k). A large share of Partner traffic is still onboarding / setup, not tools or reporting.

Verified · watch

Link & Content Tools almost unused

Partner Tools: 2,143 accounts — 1.0% of actives — averaging 1m 28s. Sample is well above the quality-gate floor. Either Tools are undiscoverable from Home/Checklist, or they are not the job most Partners came to do.

Verified · high friction

Find Brands U-turns

Discover: 56,867 accounts, 519k U-turns. Find Brands: 43,147 accounts, 261k U-turns. Same “search / back out / retry” shape as Brand Marketplace.

Verified

Retention is the Partner problem

93% of registered Partner accounts did not visit in 30 days. Of those who did, only 15% are still active a month later. Unique monthly accounts jumped 157k → 216k while sessions got slightly thinner.

Recommendations

Do thisBecause
1If you are working Partner Home or onboarding, start from Checklist → Dashboard → Find Brands. That is the actual path.59% of actives hit Checklist; 46% hit Home; Tools is 1%.
2Before investing in Link & Content Tools chrome, measure discoverability from Home/Checklist — or confirm the JTBD is elsewhere (invites, profile, applications).Tools 2,143 accounts vs Invitations 29.7k and Edit Profile 30.7k.
3Partner Find Brands deserves the same IA / empty-state / filter scrutiny as Brand Marketplace.261k U-turns on Find Brands; 519k in Discover overall.
4Watch August’s unique-account surge in the September snapshot before celebrating activation.+37.5% accounts vs prior 30 days; month-1 retention still 15%.
