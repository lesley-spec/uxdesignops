# Integrity — Test / test-plan

## Verified vs AI

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Verified** | Success criteria, quotes, or metrics from one-pager, epic, PRD, or prior research pack |
| **AI interpretation** | Draft tasks, probes, synthetic pre-test findings — review before use |

In FigJam / Confluence / Slack, distinguish AI content (magenta / dashed treatment in the DesignOps board legend).

## Human gate

Review AI study summaries and session guides **before stakeholders**. Creating an Outset **draft** after designer confirm is allowed. Do not auto-publish, launch recruitment, or treat draft interviews as Verified.

## Loop-back to Empathize

After **real** sessions:

- Use `synthesize-research` (single study corpus) or `user-research` for gate briefs
- Write **Verified** findings to Confluence/Jira as next Empathize input
- Do **not** feed synthetic pre-test output into the research pack as confirmed evidence

## PII

Do not paste participant emails, names, or raw session IDs into shared docs unless the destination is approved. Prefer aggregate counts and anonymized quotes in study plans.

## Marketplace audiences

Classify as **Brand**, **Partner**, or **Shared**. Task scripts must match the recruited side — use persona skills for realism checks.
