# Interview guides

For SPRINT follow-ups and DEEP fieldwork — the format where a human can follow a
thread an AI cannot. Everything here protects that advantage.

---

## Shape

```
WARM-UP               ~5m    Who are you, what does a normal week look like
ARTEFACTS             ~10m   Show me the thing. Their screen, their spreadsheet
TASK                  ~15m   Do the thing, while talking
THE SURPRISE           ~5m   Whatever they raised that you did not expect
CLOSE                  ~5m   The one question asked of everyone
```

**Artefacts and tasks before opinions.** Ask what someone thinks and you get a
considered answer about an imagined version. Ask them to show you and you get
the workaround, the forty browser tabs, the spreadsheet that replaced your
feature.

## Writing probes

Written down, so two interviewers get comparable data:

```
After they describe the workflow, ask:
  "When you say you export it — from where, and into what?"
Then:
  "How often does that happen?"
```

Vague probes ("ask about their workflow") produce a different conversation per
participant, and the sample becomes a collection of n=1s.

Keep a short **do-not-ask** list: the leading question someone keeps slipping
into, the feature name you want them to arrive at unprompted.

## The rules that bite hardest here

From [`prompts.md`](../../research-planner/references/prompts.md):

- **Test the thing, not the idea.** A prototype on screen, not a description
- **No hypothetical futures** without a stimulus
- **No yes/no comprehension** — ask them to recall or do
- Silence is a tool. Count to five before filling it

## The close

One question, asked of everyone, every session:

> *"What is the one thing that would make this better for you?"*

Comparable across the sample, and the answers rarely match the backlog.

## During

Note **tone shifts, workarounds and say/do contradictions** as they happen — the
transcript loses them. "Said it was easy while taking ninety seconds to find it"
is a finding no transcript records.

Record with consent, one note per session within the hour, and stop at the
3-participant pass ([`rigor.md`](../../research-planner/references/rigor.md))
before running the rest.
