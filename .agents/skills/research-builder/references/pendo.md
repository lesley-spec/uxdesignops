# Pendo guides

In-product, large n, reaches people you could never recruit. It also interrupts
someone mid-task, which is the price and the reason to keep it short.

---

## When it is the right instrument

**Use it** for one narrow question about the surface someone is on right now ·
to put a number behind a qual finding · to reach the silent majority who never
answer a recruitment email.

**Don't** for anything needing explanation, follow-up, or a task. That is an
interview, and a guide pretending to be one annoys people and returns nothing.

## Shape

**Two or three questions. Three is the ceiling.**

```
Q1   The one thing you need          MC or 1–5 rating
Q2   Why, only if Q1 says so         MULTI-SELECT, capped
Q3   Open, optional, last            TEXT — expect most to skip
```

Target by **segment and page**, never site-wide. A guide on every page is noise
and trains people to dismiss the next one unread.

## Writing it

- Ask about **the thing they are doing right now** — that immediacy is the whole
  advantage over a survey
- Short options, four or five, no jargon
- No yes/no comprehension question
  ([`prompts.md`](../../research-planner/references/prompts.md)) — nobody admits
  confusion to a popup
- Say how long it takes, and be right

## Before it goes live

- [ ] Response cap and end date set
- [ ] Segment and page targeting, not site-wide
- [ ] Frequency capped so one person is asked once
- [ ] Dismissible, and dismissal is not counted as a response
- [ ] **A human has signed off** — this is customer-facing

Never publish to live traffic from a draft. The skill builds; a person ships.

## Reading the results

**Expect single-digit response rates**, and read them accordingly.

The trap is the **satisfaction skew**: people answer when annoyed or delighted,
and the middle is missing. A Pendo result is a strong positive signal and a weak
negative one — absence of complaint is not evidence that something works.

Record it as **MEDIUM at best**: one instrument, self-selected sample, however
large the n. Large n from a skewed frame is still one source.
