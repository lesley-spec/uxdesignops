# The maintenance note

Short, dated, sent to the team. It is the artefact that proves the review
happened and the thing people actually read.

Post it where the team is, and link it from the Frame.

---

```markdown
# Frame maintenance — [quarter]
Reviewed [date] by [name] · [n] claims, [n] needs

## What changed in what we believe
- [claim] — [what moved, and what moved it]

## What we retired
- [claim] — [what changed, and when]

## What we still cannot explain
- [the thing the evidence does not settle]

## Where we are blind
[the coverage cells that matter, not all of them — name the ones with a live
decision behind them]

## What to research next
1. [need or question] — [why it ranks here: what rests on it, how weak it is]
2. …
3. …

## Numbers
| | This quarter | Last |
|---|---|---|
| Claims re-verified | | |
| Claims retired | | |
| Over 6 months | | |
| Requests closed as already-answered | | |

## What we would do differently
[one line, honestly]
```

---

## Writing it

**Lead with what changed, not with what was reviewed.** Nobody needs the
process; they need to know which belief they were working from is no longer
safe.

**Name the decisions at risk.** "We retired the claim about the assets page"
matters less than "the Q1 nav work was partly justified by a claim we have now
retired."

**Keep "what we still cannot explain."** It is the section that earns trust, and
it is where next quarter's DEEP research comes from.

**One line on what you would do differently.** A review that never produces one
is a review being performed rather than done.
