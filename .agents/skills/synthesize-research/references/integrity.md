# Integrity — single-corpus synthesis

- Verified quotes only from the provided corpus, with participant id and
  timestamp or link. A quote you cannot point at is not evidence.
- **Ceiling confidence = MEDIUM** until triangulated with another source. One
  corpus is one source however large N is; a large skewed frame is still skewed.
- Every theme ships with its disconfirming evidence, or it ships marked
  "counter-evidence not checked".
- Frequency and importance are recorded as separate numbers, never merged into
  "how strong" a theme is.
- Language stays inside the sample: counts, not percentages, below n≈30.
- Narrative and implications are marked **AI interpretation**; observations are
  not.
- Designer confirms problem worth solving. The skill themes; a person decides.
- Minimize PII in shared outputs.
