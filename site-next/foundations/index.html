Foundations

The pipeline

Five phases. Each one ends when a person confirms the decision. The agents stop there.

Phases

1

Empathize

What we know from the Frame, product data, and studies.

Gate: this is a problem worth solving.

2

Define

A one-pager and a linked UX epic.

Gate: publish.

3

Ideate

A few directions, then a critique against the spec.

Gate: a chosen direction.

4

Prototype

A Brand or Partner screen you can click.

Gate: ready to test.

5

Test

A study plan. Findings go back to Empathize.

Gate: recruit.

Join in the middle

If you already have research, a one-pager, mocks, or a prototype, start at that phase. The pipeline asks what you have before it starts over.

Brand and Partner

These are different products. Say which one. Shared work names both. Screens and this documentation use Styleguide V2.

What every agent keeps

  • Label what is verified and what is an interpretation. Do not invent quotes or numbers.
  • Brand and Partner stay on their own shells.
  • Critique a direction against the spec before prototyping it.
  • Stop at the human gate. Publishing, recruiting, and a chosen direction wait for a person.
